# FortID CTF 2026 — Post-event archive

A sanitized historical record of challenge progress and methods from the ended event. This repository is not a challenge client or a place to submit answers.

## Contents

- [`PROGRESS.md`](PROGRESS.md) records catalog-backed credit and cautious, high-level notes on solved and unfinished challenges.
- [`tools/README.md`](tools/README.md) documents four local-only capabilities: ELF call-graph fingerprinting, incremental PMAF rendering, QR-overlay SAT search, and offline wallet-address derivation. All inputs are caller-supplied; no live-service access is part of this archive.

Raw handouts, challenge binaries, caches, flags, credentials, cookies, tokens, live instance details, identity-linked OSINT, and executable or unpatched exploit material are intentionally excluded.

The challenge-method and unfinished-work notes in `PROGRESS.md` are conservative paraphrases of the contemporaneous diary, not independent reproductions. A missing catalog credit means only that this saved catalog records none; it does not mean a challenge was impossible.

## Rules and eligibility

AI assistance was used during challenge work. The contemporaneous organizer rules were found later and prohibited generative AI for challenge analysis, solution code or payloads, and guidance. This archive does not represent the participation or results as rules-compliant or prize-eligible.
